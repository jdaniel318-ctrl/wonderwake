BLACK, WHITE AND GRAYS
Colors opens with a dedicated row: pure black, charcoal, dark gray, mid gray, light gray and pure white. The black-to-white slider selects any of 256 neutral shades directly. Keep this gray adds a non-preset shade to My colors. The color wheel now has an Intensity slider as well as Brightness, so tints do not require finding the exact center of the wheel.

SHADING
Open Pen and choose Soft brush for feathered shadows or highlights. Each drawing tool has its own size and opacity. Low opacity gives light passes; 100% gives solid color at the center. Successive strokes build up shading. For a crisp opaque line use Pen at 100%. Marker starts at 38% and Pencil at 78%, preserving the familiar defaults; both can now be adjusted. Tool settings stay with the explorer.

LAYERS
Open Layers on the dock. Add up to six layers; the selected row is where new strokes go. Layers at the top cover those below. Rename, hide/show, lock/unlock, move up/down, or adjust the whole layer's opacity. Eraser removes marks only from the selected layer and reveals the lower layers. Cream paper remains underneath all layers.

Remove this layer asks before deleting marks. Undo/Redo controls strokes, not layer management. Starting New paper resets to one empty layer. All layers, including hidden ones, remain in a saved picture and progress backup. A PNG is the visible flattened picture.

KEEP WORKING ON A PICTURE
Gallery now has Edit picture. It opens an editable copy. Save updates that same picture instead of filling another gallery slot. Starting New paper while editing detaches the old picture; the next saved drawing becomes a new gallery picture. A deliberately emptied existing picture can also be saved. Save or Download before leaving unfinished work.

THREE DEVICE CHECKS
1. Draw black, gray and white marks with Pen at 100%. Try the gray slider. Check that the first outside tap only closes the menu.
2. Draw a background, add a details layer, then shade with Soft brush around 20–30% opacity. Hide/show the layer and erase a small part; the background should stay intact.
3. Save, open Gallery and Edit picture. Confirm the layers and shading remain. Save an edit, reopen the same explorer and check it again.

COMPATIBILITY
Same address, database and learners. Existing pictures and progress files remain readable; old artwork is not converted until opened for editing and explicitly saved. New layered pictures require Shadecraft or later. Older recovery builds cannot read all new data. Do not clear website data.

The large paper, 2,000-stroke / 50,000-point allowance and 12-picture gallery remain. Six layers bound tablet memory use. This is still local family preview; no cloud sync, fleet bootstrap or Engine deployment is included. recovery/ holds the previous application ZIP, not learner data. Native Safari/Pencil feel and memory pressure require the device check.
