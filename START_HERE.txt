WONDERWAKE06 — OPENPAPER · 0.5.0

1. On the existing Wonderwake page, keep a progress copy for each learner you want backed up. Save any current paper to its gallery first.
2. Unzip this package. Upload all top-level files INSIDE Wonderwake06-Openpaper to the existing wonderwake repository root, replacing matching files. Leave verification/ and recovery/ on your device; neither folder is required to run the game.
3. Once GitHub Pages finishes, refresh the existing Wonderwake page once. Check for OPENPAPER · FAMILY PREVIEW 0.5.0. Choose the same learner, then Paint a picture.

YOUR STUDIO
Islands, Gallery and Save stay at the top. The small dock holds Colour, Pen, Undo, Redo and More. Tap Colour for twelve swatches. Tap Pen for pen/pencil/marker/eraser and thickness. Tap the paper or close the tray to continue. More holds PNG download, finger drawing, new paper and shapes.

BIGGER PAPER, MORE ROOM TO CREATE
The paper uses the available screen shape. Rotation fits the original paper without stretching your marks. Up to 2,000 strokes / 50,000 sampled points per picture; 12 saved pictures per learner. New paper is temporary until Save succeeds. Download is another way to keep a PNG.

KEEP YOUR EXISTING WORK
Same hosted address, database, learner IDs and galleries. Existing saved pictures and progress exports are supported. Do not clear website data. New Openpaper paintings require this version or later; older builds cannot read all the new gallery data.

recovery/ contains the previous application ZIP, not learner records. Keep progress exports separately. No files are deployed by downloading this package. The Black Flag Engine is unchanged.

DEVICE CHECK
Draw a detailed picture; switch colours/tools; Save and open Gallery. Confirm the screen stays still and the picture looks right on your actual Safari/Pencil. Automated checks are described in VERIFY.txt.
