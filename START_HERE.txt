THE LANTERN TRAIL
Three connected puzzles lead to a lantern lighting up, with three island routes to explore.
Bridge Bay: count the spaces and fit a plank into each gap. Drag a plank onto the picture, or tap a plank and then a Gap button. Filled gaps can be tapped without a selected plank to return the piece. A wrong fit is a chance to try another idea.
Firefly Trail: draw a route from START, through the three golden sparks, to FINISH. Go around the water. You can also tap waypoints; the game joins them. Follow trail lets your explorer walk the route you made. Keyboard: focus the scene, use arrow keys to move the cursor, and Space to add a point.
Lantern Hollow: move the lantern to discover shapes under the dark layer. Match the pictured three-shape code using the discovered buttons. Keyboard arrows move the light. Light lantern finishes the island.

UNDO, CLUES AND SAVING
The current puzzle has one compact set of controls: Undo, Reset, Clue and the next action. Reset can be undone. Clues show one possible approach; using them does not take anything away. There are no timers or lives. Reduced motion and optional sound follow the explorer's existing preferences. After using Pencil, Use finger safely switches back to finger play.
Committed moves and completed puzzle checkpoints save with the current explorer. Wait for Trail saved on this device before refreshing or closing the browser. If Not saved appears, keep the page open and use Keep a progress copy on the islands. In-page progress remains available for export. Canceled unfinished gestures are discarded; committed moves remain.
The first completion of each island awards 10 discovery sparks. Replay is welcome but does not repeatedly award those sparks. Quest play does not automatically increase independent math or spelling mastery.
