WHAT CHANGED
Explorers take little steps, sway and face their route. Golden fireflies gather behind an accepted trail; fitted planks and newly found symbols sparkle. An 800 ms lantern dissolve connects Bridge Bay, Firefly Trail, Lantern Hollow, the lit island and the next island. The playing surface and header stay in place. Skip animation is available during travel and transitions. Reduced motion or turning Gentle motion off completes effects immediately.
An unfinished or rejected trail now leaves the explorer at the yellow START circle. Solving and saving a puzzle happens before its celebration. Leaving during an effect resumes at the saved puzzle checkpoint.
