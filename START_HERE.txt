COLORS AND TOOLS
The dock now says Colors. Open Colors, choose Make a color, move around the wheel and adjust Brightness. Add color puts it in My colors and selects it on the dock. An optional hex field lets you match an exact color. Your 12 default swatches remain; each explorer can keep 16 custom colors. Removing a swatch never changes a saved picture.

Open Pen to choose Pen, Pencil, Marker or Eraser. The size slider runs from 1 to 40 and remembers a separate setting for each tool and explorer. A small preview shows the line changing. Pencil is finer and Marker broader than Pen at the same setting.

Tap outside an open menu to close it. That entire tap or drag only closes the menu; after the short transition and lifting your fingers, the next gesture can draw. Done and Escape are also available. Menu changes leave the paper in place.

KEEP CREATING
The larger paper and 2,000-stroke / 50,000-point allowance remain. Each learner has 12 gallery slots. Save and Gallery stay at the top. More contains PNG download, new paper, finger drawing and shapes. New paper is temporary until saved.

KEEP YOUR EXISTING WORK
Same hosted address, database, learner IDs and galleries. Existing progress exports remain readable. Custom colors and sizes are included in new progress copies. Do not clear website data. New Prism settings and variable-width drawings require Prism or later; older builds cannot read all new data.

recovery/ contains the previous application ZIP, not learner records. Keep progress exports separately. Downloading this package does not deploy it. Wonderwake remains independent; the Black Flag Engine is unchanged.

BECCA'S THREE CHECKS
1. Make a custom color, add it, and draw with it. Try a different size for each tool.
2. Open Colors or Pen, then touch and drag on the paper. The menu should close without a mark; the next gesture should draw. The paper should stay still.
3. Save the picture, open Gallery, then reopen Wonderwake and choose the same explorer. Check the picture, My colors and tool sizes.
