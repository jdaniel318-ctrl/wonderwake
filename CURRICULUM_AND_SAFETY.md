# Wonderwake learning contract and expansion map

## Scope: seeds now, not a completed school curriculum

The build contains original practice templates across approximate K–12 content bands. Each related standards reference is a curriculum connection, not proof of complete alignment or coverage. No assessment specialist or independent educator has reviewed this release. Automatic challenge advancement is not school placement, grade certification or a diagnosis.

Math content presently has 30 limited templates: counting; quantity comparison; addition to 10; subtraction to 10; polygon names; addition to 20; tens/ones; addition within 100; equal groups; division; pictured fractions; equivalent fractions; decimal addition; rectangle perimeter; rectangle area; like-denominator fraction addition; multiplicative scaling; percent; signed addition; one-step reduction of two-step linear equations; positive integer powers; triangle angle sum; linear-function substitution; Pythagorean triples; factoring positive-root quadratics; sine as a ratio; mean; simple probability; geometric sequences; vertex-form x-coordinate.

This omits many required tasks. For example, recognizing a shape does not teach all spatial reasoning; a right-triangle ratio is not a full trigonometry course; evaluating a function is not complete algebra; no proofs, broad free-response reasoning, multistep assessment or calculus curriculum are included. Counting seeds currently use 1–10, not the full range of the referenced kindergarten standards.

The 12 spelling banks cover short words, blends, consonant teams, silent e, vowel teams, inflectional endings, suffixes, prefixes, roots, academic words, difficult spelling patterns and technical words. Word banks alone are not a systematic reading program. Speech is word narration, not a reviewed phoneme audio library. Daily connected reading, explicit phonological-awareness instruction, handwriting instruction and contextual writing need fuller future content. Tracing guides do not recognize or grade a child's letter formation.

## Expansion without replacing the vessel

Content, progression, persistence and rendering are separate modules. New skill packs should declare stable skill IDs, prerequisites, examples, solution methods, answer validators, accessibility alternatives and review dates. Do not represent a new menu label as completed curriculum. Future comprehensive mathematics, geometry, reading/writing, science and other subjects require independent content review and their own checks. Science and social studies are not playable modules in this release.

Next educational milestone: a coherent, educator-reviewed early-learning unit with varied representations and authored feedback, plus a deeper older-player reasoning mission. Preserve separate mathematics and spelling readiness. Introduce a validated placement approach only after collecting appropriate consented evidence; do not infer a child's overall ability from a short game.

## Design rules

Academic challenge, movement demands and presentation remain separate. No speed penalty, leaderboard of children's performance, loss of earned creations, streak shame, paid advantage or mandatory reward grinding. Free creative play is always accessible. No child is pushed into public sharing, a microphone, or a real-name account.

## Before production child accounts or school use

The static preview is not gated and can be visited by anyone if published. A production rollout needs an explicit privacy/consent/retention/deletion design, parent-managed authentication, applicable legal review, accessible-device testing, secure server-scoped learner authorization, verified backup and restore, and a dedicated hosting/origin decision. Existing local profiles are convenience slots, not authenticated identities. No COPPA/FERPA compliance or school-data-security certification is claimed.
